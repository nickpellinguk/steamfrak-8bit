Moving platform

One per screen: the ball, 8 px wide and 6 lines tall, between From and To (its left edge). Two-way pauses at each end; one way pauses at its start, carries the Trogg across, pauses, and is gone for a second before it starts again. Speed: pixels per frame.

View

Keys

Clicking an existing object selects it, whatever the tool; the drawing tools only create on empty space. Arrow keys move the selection (3 px for ladders and items, 4 px (a playfield block) for platforms; up/down a row, or 6 lines for platforms). Platforms are always 6 lines (3 rows) tall, with tops on 6-line boundaries. Shift + arrows resize: a platform's width (left/right), a ladder's length. Delete removes it. Click the gutter to inspect a row.
