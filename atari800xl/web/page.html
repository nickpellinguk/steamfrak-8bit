What this is

The 8x4 screen demo for the Atari 800XL port: the C64 port's levels on ANTIC mode 2 rows 4 lines high. Each pair of rows shows glyph lines 4–7 and then 0–3 of its characters, so every character code holds two 4-line glyphs.

Trogg, balloons and daggers on A_1: outlines drawn into spare characters with AND and OR tables, 8 pixels a byte, at any even x and any y, clipped where they go off the screen. The joystick walks and climbs Trogg, and fire shows his yoyo pose. Balloons rise from below and daggers fly in from above.

The 4-line row test draws a staircase: each row of a pair should show four steps. Five steps, or rows 8 lines high, mean the timing is off.
