What this is

Frak! on an Enterprise 128: the CPC 6128 version's Z80 code, on a 320 x 256 four-colour screen, double-buffered and scrolled through Nick's line parameter tables. It starts on the attract screen: press Space for the menus, move with O and K (or the arrow keys), choose with Space, and play.

Collect all the keys. The yoyo knocks meanies off and pops balloons and daggers; touching any of them kills Trogg. The game runs at 50 ticks a second however long the drawing takes.

There's no sound yet. The machine starts without the Enterprise's ROMs: the page puts the game's files in memory as EXOS would have loaded them, with a public-domain font in place of EXOS's for the menus' text.

The emulator is ep128emu by István Varga (GPL-2.0), its emulation compiled to WebAssembly, with the video RAM's wait states, so it runs at the real machine's speed. The font is Daniel Hepper's public-domain font8x8. The game: enterprise128/ in the steamfrak-8bit repository.
